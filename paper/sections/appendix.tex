The exploratory production-like shallow-water comparisons are the main qualification on this evidence. Across three production-like cases, trajectory relative $L_2$ against GeoClaw is $0.414$--$0.537$ for Hydrostatic and $0.231$--$0.259$ for MUSCL-HR. The numerical program therefore supports the specific implementation and refinement checks needed for the benchmark, but does not establish pointwise equivalence, general boundary independence, or event-scale validity.

\paragraph{Boussinesq dispersion diagnostic.}
Small-amplitude periodic sinusoidal waves are propagated on a $256 \times 32$ constant-depth grid for modes $1, 2, 4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32$ at $\Delta t = 1.25 \times 10 ^ {-4}$ and final time $1.5$. Fourier phase tracking is compared with the discrete relation
\[
    \omega ^ 2 =
    \frac{g H k_d ^ 2}{1 + \alpha_B H ^ 2 k_d ^ 2},
    \qquad
    k_d = \frac{2 \sin(k \Delta x / 2)}{\Delta x}.
\]
The $\alpha_B = 0$ branch approaches the non-dispersive shallow-water relation, while $\alpha_B = 1/3$ slows higher modes according to the implemented weakly dispersive operator. Figure~\ref{fig:app-bouss-dispersion} shows the phase-speed comparison, and Table~\ref{tab:app-bouss-dispersion} gives the maximum errors and linear-solver status. This is a local implementation diagnostic, not external-solver or event validation.

\paragraph{Conditional seeded-window diagnostics.}
The windowed models predict in $K = 5$ frame blocks after receiving the first true target frame, then are scored on the remaining $49$ frames. They solve an easier conditional rollout task than a direct 50-frame prediction and are therefore reported separately. On the ordinary Hydrostatic test split, Window-FNO-5 obtains denormalized benchmark-scale relative $L_2 = 0.064$ and Window-F-FNO-5 obtains $0.053$ (Table~\ref{tab:app-window}). The corresponding direct FNO and F-FNO values are $0.244$ and $0.190$, respectively, but the tasks are not like-for-like.
