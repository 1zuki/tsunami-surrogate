\section*{Data and code availability}
The source code, configuration files, data-generation and evaluation scripts, plotting scripts, and the result files (JSON/CSV) underlying every table and figure in this paper are publicly available at \url{https://github.com/1zuki/tsunami-surrogate}.

The processed benchmark datasets (model-ready train/validation/test arrays for the hydrostatic, MUSCL-HR, and Boussinesq references), together with the scenario manifests, train/validation/test scenario identifiers, normalization statistics, and checksums, are archived with a persistent identifier at \texttt{10.5281/zenodo.20974604}. The full raw rollout archive ($\approx$ 300\,GB) can be regenerated from the released scripts and configuration files; a mirror is additionally available at \url{https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/16hC9hTBBTw_xygkEtc45bYrM2mjrJ5TU?usp=sharing}.

The real-bathymetry diagnostic crops were derived from the GEBCO\_2026 Grid \citep{gebco2026}; we acknowledge the GEBCO Bathymetric Compilation Group 2026 and the NERC EDS British Oceanographic Data Centre NOC for providing the dataset.

Trained model checkpoints are not redistributed in full, but the released
repository includes the resolved configurations, random seeds, the checkpoint-selection rule, the final per-model metrics, and the training histories needed to reproduce the reported results.
