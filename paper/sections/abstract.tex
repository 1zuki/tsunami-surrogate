\begin{abstract}
We study neural-operator surrogates for tsunami-like wave propagation in a controlled synthetic benchmark based on two-dimensional shallow-water simulations. Each scenario pairs a procedurally generated bathymetry field with a synthetic initial displacement source, and the same scenario can be advanced by multiple numerical references to separate surrogate error from solver-fidelity effects. The primary experiments evaluate a Fourier Neural Operator (FNO) against convolutional and recurrent baselines under matched train/test protocols. We focus on solver fidelity, inference speedup, robustness under distribution shift, including held-out bathymetry and source families, cross-resolution transfer from coarse to finer grids, and ensemble-based uncertainty quality. The benchmark is designed for research on fast forward emulation of solver-generated wavefields, not for operational tsunami warning or site-specific hazard assessment.
\end{abstract}
